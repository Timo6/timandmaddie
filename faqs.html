Frequently asked questions

What to wear

Please come dressed ready to celebrate, bringing comfortable footwear for dancing is strongly encouraged and you might want something warm for the evening.

We don’t mind if you match the bridesmaids but in case you do they are in pale blue.

Food and drink

The wedding breakfast will be an afternoon tea with a selection of sandwiches, sweet treats and scones.

There will be a selection of freshly made pizzas in the evening, and a bar for you to buy drinks and ice creams.

Please let us know any dietary requirements when you RSVP so we can make sure we cater for you.

Timings

Please arrive by 1.45pm for the service, and check your invitation for other timings, or contact us with any questions.

Locations and parking

See How to find us for the locations and parking suggestions.

Accessibility

There is disabled parking at both venues, please let us know if you would like a space reserved. Both venues have level access, though Basing House is a historic building and the floor may be uneven.
