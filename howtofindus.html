How to find us

The ceremony will be held at St Mary's Church, Eastrop, RG21 7PZ, Please arrive by 1:45pm. Due to the limited parking at the church please make use of the paid car parks in the town centre (we recommend the Red Lion car park, RG21 7LX, or the Festival Place car park, RG21 7BF, both a short walk away).

Please then join us at The Tudor Great Barn at Basing House, RG24 7BH to carry on the celebrations. The car park for Basing House can be found off Barton's Lane, RG24 8AE. Please then follow the path as it takes you past Barton's Mill Pub and along the water to the Great Barn. You are welcome to leave your car in the car park overnight and collect it after 10am on the Sunday.
