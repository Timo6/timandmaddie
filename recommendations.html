Recommendations

Things to do

Activites

Knights Realm adventure golf is a 7 minute drive from St Mary's. See if you can beat our best score!

The Vyne is a lovely National Trust property. Last entry is at 4pm, so this might be better Saturday morning or Sunday.

Places to eat and drink

Bartons Mill and The Mill House are two waterside pubs we enjoy.

Festival Place has a number of cafes and resturants.

Walks

Basingstoke Canel is lovely to stroll along, there's even a castle to see!

Basingstoke Common, Crabtree Plantation, and War Memorial Park are all nice green spaces to wander around, with play areas too.
